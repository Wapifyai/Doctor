"What is Foot & Ankle Pain?"

Foot and ankle pain refers to any discomfort felt in the lower limbs, often caused by injury, inflammation, or structural issues.

The feet carry the entire weight of the bodywhich is why pain hinders mobility. Our goal is to enable you to start taking steps again.

Why Does it Happen?

Understanding the root causes of your lower limb discomfort.

Sports & Physical Impact

High-impact activities like running or jumping put immense pressure on the heel and ligaments, often leading to acute sprains.

Improper Footwear

Wearing shoes without proper arch support causes the foot's natural structure to collapse, leading to Plantar Fasciitis.
