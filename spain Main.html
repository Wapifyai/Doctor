"What exactly is Spine Pain?"

Spine pain can be felt in the neck, back, or legs (Sciatica). It is often caused by damaged discs, nerve compression, or structural misalignment.

Spine is our body's main support system, which is why pain affects both stability and movement.

Why Do Spine Issues Happen?

Understanding the root causes of your spinal discomfort.

Mechanical Stress

Poor posture, lifting heavy weights, aur sedentary lifestyle spine par extra pressure dalte hain, jisse structural imbalance hota hai.

Age-related Wear

Umar ke saath spinal discs apni hydration kho dete hain. Yeh disc degeneration nerves ko compress kar ke dard paida karti hai.
